Describe

Pokémon, years, sets, grader, grade, language, price, fair-value discount, free text. Anything you can type.

02

Launch

A token is created on pump.fun. Zero contracts of ours, no allocation. The strategy's wallet is the creator, so creator fees accrue to it on every trade.

03

Scan

Every minute the treasury reads live Collector Crypt listings, normalizes them, and ranks the matches by your rules.

04

Buy

When it can pay, it buys with its own wallet. The graded card stays in Collector Crypt's insured vault; the strategy holds the NFT, keeps it or flips it at a markup.
